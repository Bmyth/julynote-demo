Weekend Pancakes
1 cup flour, 1 egg, 3/4 cup milk, 1 tbsp sugar, pinch of salt, 1 tsp baking powder.
Mix wet and dry separately, then combine — don't overmix. Cook on medium, flip once bubbles form on top.
